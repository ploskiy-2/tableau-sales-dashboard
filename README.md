# Sales Performance Dashboards

Two Tableau dashboards built to help sales managers, executives, and marketing teams analyze sales performance and customer behavior.

## Sales Dashboard
- **KPI Overview**: total sales, profit, and quantity (current vs. previous year)
- **Sales Trends**: monthly KPI trends with highest/lowest months highlighted
- **Product Subcategory Comparison**: sales vs. profit by subcategory, year-over-year
- **Weekly Trends**: weekly sales & profit vs. average, with above/below average weeks highlighted

## Customer Dashboard
- **KPI Overview**: total customers, sales per customer, and order count (current vs. previous year)
- **Customer Trends**: monthly KPI trends with highest/lowest months highlighted
- **Customer Distribution**: customers segmented by number of orders
- **Top 10 Customers by Profit**: rank, orders, sales, profit, and last order date

## Features
- Year selector for exploring historical data
- Easy navigation between dashboards
- Interactive charts for filtering
- Filters by product (category, subcategory) and location (region, state, city)
